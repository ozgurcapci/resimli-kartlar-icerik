## Gece fotoğraf kuyruğu

> Photorealistic photograph, shot on a full-frame camera with a prime lens, natural low light at dusk or night, warm glow from lamps and candles, soft and calm color grading, high detail, serene and spiritual mood. The main subject sits low in the frame, grounded near the bottom edge, and does not rise above the middle of the image. The entire upper half is calm, open space with soft, out-of-focus or plain dark tones and no busy detail, so a greeting text can be written there later. The image continues naturally to every edge: no blank strips, no borders, no frame. No text, no letters, no signs, no calligraphy, no Arabic script, no watermark, no logo. No visible faces. No crescent symbols, no lens flare.
