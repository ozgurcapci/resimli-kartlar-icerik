# Gece kartları: Flow istemleri (Nano Banana Pro, 9:16, x2)

20 istem: 1–10 suluboya (gece mavisi zemin), 11–20 gerçekçi gece fotoğrafı. Her istem konu cümlesi + ortak kuyruk.
Tema: iyi geceler; ay, yıldız, fener, mum, ıhlamur, uyuyan kedi, sessiz şehir. Ay/yıldız serbest ama ay küçük ve yanda (yazı alanının ortasında değil). Kişi yok.

## Suluboya kuyruğu (her istemin sonuna)

> Full-bleed vertical watercolor painting with delicate gold-leaf accents, Ottoman-Turkish Islamic aesthetic, serene and elegant, hand-painted on textured cotton paper. Deep night-blue watercolor background, softly graded from #22324A at the top to #121C2C at the bottom, with a few tiny gold-leaf stars. The background color and paper texture continue evenly all the way to every edge of the image: no blank strips, no bands, no margins, no light edges at the top or bottom. The main subject sits low in the image, grounded near the bottom edge, and does not rise above the middle of the image. The entire upper half is calm, open night sky with only soft washes and a few small stars, so a greeting text can be written there later. Any moon is small and placed toward one side, never in the center of the upper half. No text, no letters, no calligraphy, no Arabic script, no numbers, no watermark, no frame or border. No lens flare.

## Fotoğraf kuyruğu (her istemin sonuna)

> Photorealistic night photograph, shot on a full-frame camera with a prime lens, natural low light, deep blue and warm amber tones, soft and calm color grading, high detail, serene and peaceful mood. The main subject sits low in the frame, grounded near the bottom edge, and does not rise above the middle of the image. The entire upper half is calm, open dark space or night sky with no busy detail, so a greeting text can be written there later. Any moon is small and placed toward one side, never in the center of the upper half. The image continues naturally to every edge: no blank strips, no borders, no frame. No text, no letters, no signs, no calligraphy, no Arabic script, no watermark, no logo. No people, no visible faces. No lens flare.
